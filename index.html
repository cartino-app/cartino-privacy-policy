Cartino Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 14, 2026

Software Center ("we", "us", or "our") develops and operates the Cartino mobile application ("Cartino" or the "App").

This Privacy Policy explains how Cartino handles information when you use the App.

1. Information We Collect

Cartino does not collect, transmit, or store personal information on our servers.

The App does not require you to create an account or provide personal information such as your name, email address, telephone number, or physical address.

2. Local Data Storage

Cartino allows you to create and manage shopping lists. The information you enter into the App is stored locally on your device.

This information is not transmitted to or stored on servers operated by Software Center.

3. Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal information with third parties.

4. Advertising and Analytics

Cartino does not contain third-party advertising and does not use third-party analytics or tracking services.

5. Purchases

The current version of Cartino does not contain in-app purchases or subscription functionality.

If you purchase Cartino through Google Play, the transaction is processed by Google Play in accordance with Google's own terms and privacy policies. Cartino does not receive or store your payment information.

6. Data Deletion

Because Cartino stores shopping list data locally on your device, you can delete this data using the deletion functionality provided within the App or by deleting the App from your device.

7. Children's Privacy

Cartino does not knowingly collect personal information from children or from any other users.

8. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time, for example when the functionality of Cartino changes. Any updated version will be published on this page with a new "Last updated" date.

9. Contact

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy or the privacy practices of Cartino, you can contact us at:

Software Center
Email: cartino.contact@gmail.com
